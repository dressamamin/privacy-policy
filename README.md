# Privacy Policy for RadioSphere

**Last updated:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the **RadioSphere: FM No Headphone** mobile application ("Application", "App", or "Service").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. RadioSphere is designed as a free utility that allows you to listen to global public radio broadcasts without requiring personal account registration.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use RadioSphere.
* **No Personal Data Collected Directly:** We do not directly collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).

### B. Device & Diagnostic Information (Automated via Third Parties)
When you use the Application, third-party services (such as Google Play Services and Google AdMob) may automatically collect certain standard technical data:
* Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
* Unique device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID).
* Approximate location (derived from IP address for localized ad serving and language selection).
* App performance diagnostics, error logs, and crash reports.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

### A. Google AdMob
We use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements (Banners, Interstitial ads, and Rewarded video ads). AdMob may use device identifiers, cookies, and network location to serve non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### B. Radio Browser Community API
The Application fetches publicly available radio stream URLs from the community-driven Radio Browser directory. When you play a radio station, your device connects directly to the third-party streaming server hosted by the respective broadcaster. We do not control or operate third-party radio stream servers.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage & Preferences
The Application stores minimal preference data locally on your device using Android Room Database and DataStore:
* Your saved favorite stations.
* Your recent listening history.
* Your sleep timer settings.
* Ad-Free VIP pass timer expiration.

This data remains strictly on your local device storage and is never uploaded to any external server. You can clear this data at any time via the in-app **Settings -> Clear Data** option or through your Android device settings.

---

## 4. Permissions Requested
* `INTERNET`: Required to stream live online radio channels.
* `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to detect network availability (Wi-Fi vs. mobile data).
* `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`: Required to continue continuous radio playback in the background and lock screen.
* `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Required on Android 13+ to display media playback controls in the notification tray.
* `WAKE_LOCK`: Prevents CPU sleep from cutting off audio streams while the screen is off.
